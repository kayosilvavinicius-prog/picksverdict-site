What it is

A review service in a box

You type one local business. The app pulls its public Google listing and reviews, shows its review gap against nearby competitors and builds the assets you'd use to pitch and serve that business.

⚡

Magic Build

One name in: a branded review page, QR code, print kit (table tent, counter card, decal), a Review Gap Report, AI reply drafts, social cards, a pitch email and a proposal, per the vendor.

★

Rating-adaptive review page

Happy customers get a short path to Google. At low scores a private box to the owner opens first, and a smaller "post a public review on Google" button stays on the same screen.

📊

Deliver and report

A recovery desk for private complaints, owner alerts, a client portal and a monthly proof report with your agency's logo.

💬

AI reply drafts

The AI writes replies to Google reviews; you copy, paste and post them yourself. It does not post to Google on its own.

🎨

Show real reviews

Social cards, review "stories", a Wall of Love page and website widgets, made word for word from real reviews.

🔌

Extras

Email requests, SMS through your own Twilio account, a Claude & ChatGPT connector and an API with webhooks, per the vendor's feature list.

Features as described on the vendor's public pages, Nov 22, 2026. We haven't run a paid account yet.

FAQ

Before you buy

Does it hide bad reviews?

No, according to the vendor. At 1–3 stars a private box to the owner opens first, and a smaller "post a public review on Google" button stays on the same screen. At 4–5 stars there's a short countdown to Google with a "stay here" option. No setting removes the public option.

Does it post replies to Google for me?

No. The AI drafts the reply and opens the review in Google; you paste and post it.

Will it make me money?

Nobody can promise that, and neither the vendor nor we do. It's a tool; results depend on whether you find and keep clients.

Which plan should I start with?

Basic at $37 is enough to test the idea with a few businesses. Upgrade once you know you'll use the extra features.

Is there a refund policy?

The vendor offers 30 days through its support desk. Read the terms at checkout before you buy.

How did you review it?

We went through the vendor's public webinar page and affiliate materials for this launch and compared them with similar local-marketing tools. We stick to what's documented and flag what to check yourself.
