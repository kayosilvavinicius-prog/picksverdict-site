What it is

One input in, a full webinar out.

EverPitch is web software sold through JVZoo. Its job is the part most people never finish: turning what you know into a presentation that plays on its own. Here's how it works, in our words:

1 · You bring

Whatever you have

A one-line idea, a guided form, a PDF, a sales page link or a past Zoom call.

2 · It writes

The script

A spoken-style talk with your offer worked in. You still need to read it and fix the tone.

3 · It designs

Slides + AI voice

73 layouts in 11 styles and 30 voices in 87 languages, per the vendor. Edit by typing what you want.

4 · It runs

On a schedule

Plays in each viewer's time zone. An AI host answers questions using only the webinar's content.

Also in the box: registration templates, reminder and follow-up emails, a replay page, Stripe or PayPal checkout and an email-tool connection. The optional "simulated audience" is labeled as simulated, per the vendor; keep it that way.

Who it's for

Great fit for some. A miss for others.

Buy it if you…
  • Coach, consult or sell courses and keep putting off the webinar.
  • Explain the same thing to every prospect and want it on autopilot.
  • Have old recordings or PDFs sitting unused.
  • Build webinars for clients (needs the Agency upgrade).
Skip it if you…
  • Want a real live event with your own face and voice.
  • Have no clear topic or offer yet; it builds webinars, not demand.
  • Sell to people who dislike AI voices.
  • Expect guaranteed sign-ups or sales. No tool can promise that.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Do I need to be on camera or speak?

No. The voice is AI-generated and the slides are designed for you. Camera features (host clips, avatar Q&A, voice cloning with consent) are in the yearly Ultra upgrade, not the main plan.

Is it a monthly subscription?

Commercial is a one-time payment during the launch, with credits that refresh monthly. Ultra and Credit Boost are yearly.

Which plan should I choose?

Commercial if you want to try it on one or two webinars. The Bundle only if you'll use the done-for-you webinars, kits and client license; it costs less than buying those upgrades separately.

Will it bring me sign-ups or sales?

It can't promise that, and neither can we. Results depend on your offer, your audience and how you drive registrations.

Can I get a refund?

The vendor states a 30-day money-back guarantee. Check the terms on the checkout page.

How did you review it?

We went through the vendor's sales page, public demo and affiliate materials and compared the plans and upgrades. We haven't run a long-term project with it yet, so we stick to what's documented and flag what to check.
