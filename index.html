EverPitch review: an independent overview before you buy

By Kayo Silva · Last updated November 16, 2026 · Written with AI assistance and checked against the vendor's public pages listed under "Sources".

Short version. EverPitch is a tool that builds a webinar for you (script, slides and an AI voice) from a topic, a document, a link or an old recording, and then runs it on a schedule. It makes the most sense for people who already know what they want to teach or sell but keep putting off the work of producing and presenting a webinar. If you need a live, on-camera event with your own face and voice, or you never use webinars, it is probably not for you.

Honest note: I have not used EverPitch on a real project yet. This page summarizes what the vendor publishes and what their public demo shows, and points out what to check before buying.

What it is

EverPitch is web software sold through JVZoo. You give it a starting point, and it produces a complete presentation:

  • Input: a one-line idea, a guided form, an uploaded PDF or document, a sales page link, or an existing recording such as a past Zoom call.
  • Output: a written script, designed slides (the vendor lists 73 layouts in 11 styles) and an AI voiceover (30 voices, 87 languages, per the vendor).
  • Editing: you change slides, styles or images by typing what you want in plain language.
  • Running it: the webinar plays in a "live room" on a schedule, shown in each visitor's time zone, and an AI host answers viewer questions out loud using only the webinar's content.
  • Around it: registration page templates, reminder and follow-up emails, a replay page, checkout through your own Stripe or PayPal, and a connection to your email tool.

There is also an optional "simulated audience" feature. The vendor says it is always disclosed as simulated. If you use it, keep it that way: presenting simulated viewers as real people would mislead your audience.

Who it is for

  • Coaches, consultants and course creators who want a webinar but avoid scripting, designing and presenting.
  • Small software or service businesses that explain the same thing to every prospect.
  • People with old recordings or PDFs they would like to turn into an evergreen presentation.
  • Agencies that build webinars for clients (that needs the Agency upgrade).

Who should skip it

  • Anyone who wants a real live event with their own face and voice.
  • People without a clear topic or offer: the tool builds the webinar, but it does not create demand.
  • Audiences that react badly to AI voices or AI-hosted Q&A.
  • Anyone expecting guaranteed sign-ups or sales. No software can promise that.

Pros

  • One-time price for the main plan during the launch, with monthly credits and no monthly fee on Commercial (per the vendor).
  • Starts from material you already have (PDF, link, recording), not only from a blank page.
  • Registration, reminders, replay and checkout are included, so fewer extra tools.
  • Many languages and voices, which helps if you sell outside English.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee, according to the vendor.

Cons

  • Credit-based: Commercial includes 3,000 credits a month. Heavy use pushes you toward the Credit Boost or upgrades.
  • Several upsells after purchase (four upgrades, one of them yearly). Decide what you need before you check out.
  • New product launched in November 2026, so there is little long-term track record.
  • AI-generated scripts still need your review for accuracy and tone.
  • An automated webinar is not the same as being there live; some audiences notice.

Questions worth asking before you buy

Do I need to appear on camera or speak?

No. The voice is AI-generated and the slides are designed for you. Camera features (host clips, avatar Q&A, voice cloning with consent) are part of the yearly Ultra upgrade, not the main plan.

Is it a monthly subscription?

Commercial is a one-time payment during the launch, with credits that refresh each month. Ultra, Credit Boost and the MegaBundle options are yearly.

Will it bring me sign-ups or sales?

It cannot promise that, and neither can I. Results depend on your offer, your audience and how you get people to register.

Can I get a refund?

The vendor states a 30-day money-back guarantee. Read the terms on the checkout page.
